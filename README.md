# Business Performance Dashboard

A portfolio-ready Databricks dashboard project focused on turning fragmented business data into clear, actionable insights for performance monitoring and decision-making.

## Project Overview
This project is a business intelligence dashboard designed to give stakeholders a unified, easy-to-read view of operational and commercial performance. Built in Databricks and presented as a dashboard, it helps teams monitor trends, identify risks, and support faster business decisions using a single source of truth.

## Business Problem Being Addressed
Organizations often face a common challenge: performance data is scattered across multiple systems, teams, and reporting formats. As a result, leaders struggle to answer simple but critical business questions such as:

- Are we meeting our business targets?
- Which teams or segments are underperforming?
- Where are the bottlenecks affecting productivity and efficiency?
- Which trends need action before they become larger issues?
- How do we compare performance across time, departments, or regions?

This creates delayed decision-making, poor visibility, manual reporting effort, and inconsistent business judgment based on incomplete information.

## Business Problem Solved
This dashboard solves that problem by consolidating key business metrics into one interactive view. It enables leaders to:

- Monitor performance in a single place
- Identify trends and anomalies quickly
- Detect underperforming areas before they impact business outcomes
- Reduce reliance on manual spreadsheet-based reporting
- Improve the speed and quality of operational and strategic decisions

By turning raw business data into a clear, decision-support layer, the project helps teams move from reactive reporting to proactive management.
